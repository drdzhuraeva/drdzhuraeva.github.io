PostPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

PostPilot uses YouTube API Services. By using PostPilot you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With your permission, PostPilot uses the youtube.upload scope to upload videos you select, with the title, description, tags and privacy setting you provide. For TikTok, it uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish videos you select.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and video metadata are stored only on the owner's own computer or server. PostPilot has no central server and does not collect analytics.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with any third party. Videos and metadata are sent only to YouTube and TikTok, as you instruct.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at Google security settings and in your TikTok account settings. Deleting the local token files removes all stored credentials.
